Who we are

A strong foundation.
A vision for tomorrow.

VIHA Group of Constructions is a construction company built on experience, hard work, dedication and a commitment to quality. Our journey began in 2009 as a labour contracting business, with a vision to grow through consistent performance, reliable execution and long-term relationships.

Over the years, we have gained experience working on construction projects for several reputed companies. This journey strengthened our understanding of workforce management, site execution, quality control and construction operations.

As our capabilities expanded, we progressed into material contracting and took on greater responsibilities. Today, VIHA Group of Constructions represents our continued commitment to dependable construction services, professional management and quality workmanship.

“Every successful project begins with trust, grows through teamwork and stands strong on quality.”
